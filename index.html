Who We Are

Your Trusted Bridge to Global Markets

AparantX is a Thane, Maharashtra-based import-export company dedicated to helping Indian manufacturers unlock international market potential. With nearly 12 years of deep corporate experience spanning sales, marketing, and the packaging industry, we bring the expertise and global network that manufacturers need to go global — without the complexity.

🌍

Global Market Access

We open doors to buyers across USA, UK, Middle East, Australia, Canada, ASEAN, and Europe, giving your products the international platform they deserve.

📦

Packaging Industry Expertise

From kraft paper bags and corrugated boxes to jute bags and food packaging — our 12+ years means we know every nuance of packaging exports and compliance.

🤝

End-to-End Export Facilitation

We handle sourcing, buyer relationships, documentation, and logistics coordination — so Indian manufacturers can focus on what they do best: manufacturing.

🏆

Campaign & Conference Procurement

Beyond packaging, we support corporate procurement needs for large events, conferences, and promotional campaigns at scale.
